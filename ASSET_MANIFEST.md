avoids visible tile repetition.

### Characters — Minh (groom) & Thủy (bride) (≈25 KB, Phase 2)
Generated by `scripts/build-wedding-characters.py` (needs `assets-extracted/`).
From the Tiny Pixel Fantasy pack it uses only bodies (Body 1/2), eyes (4) and
hair (Minh: Rear 2_5 + Front 3_5; Thủy: Rear 7_5 bun + Front 3_5). Top 2, Top 7
and Bottom 4 are used **only as invisible masks** for torso, sleeves and legs. The
suit, shirt, tie, gown, sash, flowers, veil and bouquet are new pixel art painted
per frame.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `images/characters/minh-groom.png/.json` | 44 frames: 4 directions × stand, blink, walk0–3, breath, nod, offer (hand), wave; + reach/hold (down, up) |
| `images/characters/thuy-bride.png/.json` | 104 frames: 4 directions × stand, blink, walk0–3, swayL/R, breath, nod, flare, raise; + reach/hold (down, up) — each with and without the bouquet (`-bq`) |

Loaded by Chapter I (Phase 3) scenes; the names are never shown to guests.
